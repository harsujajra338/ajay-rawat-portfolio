01 — THE STORY · कहानी

From a village near Pushkar
to a life shaped in sand.

Ajay Rawat grew up in Ganahera near Pushkar, where sand was first a playground — long before it became a profession.

He has recalled playing in the dunes while his mother worked in the fields. Around class 10, a proper Lord Ganesh sculpture created during Ganesh Chaturthi became an early turning point.

There was no formal roadmap. The work improved through repetition, curiosity and persistence. Today the same medium carries stories of Rajasthan, national memory, spirituality, sport, social awareness and public life.

“Everything in life is temporary.” — Ajay Rawat, on why he continues to make art that will eventually disappear.
